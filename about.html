Moving Ideas from Concept to Store.

Founded in Dhaka in 2019, Drēbes Sourcing Company is a design-led apparel sourcing and supply chain management company dedicated to serving international brands with innovative, sustainable, and efficient manufacturing solutions.

Our global manufacturing ecosystem combines creative design, advanced product development, responsible sourcing, and data-driven production management—allowing our partners to stay ahead in an ever-changing marketplace.

We don't simply manufacture garments.
We build supply chain solutions.

Our Mission

To simplify apparel sourcing through creativity, responsible manufacturing, and efficient supply chain management while maintaining the highest standards of quality, compliance, and customer satisfaction.

Our Vision

To become a globally recognized apparel sourcing partner by delivering innovative products, sustainable solutions, and exceptional customer value.
